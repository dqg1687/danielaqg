About This Design

This banner features a beautiful gradient background with the word "Serene" centered in elegant typography. The menu items are positioned against the banner in the top-right corner, creating a clean and modern design.

The menu items have a subtle glassmorphism effect and respond to hover interactions, providing visual feedback to users. The design is fully responsive and will adapt to different screen sizes.
